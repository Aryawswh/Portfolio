Project Description

This project involves the design-and-build execution of an LNG terminal relocation facility at BMTH Development Area II. I work as an Electrical & Instrument Site Engineer responsible for executing on-site electrical and instrumentation works within an large-scale EPC environment. My role focuses on ensuring installation quality, documentation accuracy, and compliance with engineering standards and safety requirements.

Scope of Responsibilities
  • Preparing and controlling inspection documentation including Material Inspection Reports (MIR) and QC reports
  • Developing and reviewing electrical and instrumentation shop drawings for field execution
  • Supervising vendor installation of electrical and instrumentation systems
  • Managing cable routing, equipment installation, and instrumentation execution
  • Conducting inspection and quality control aligned with engineering standards and safety procedures
Site Execution Environment

Daily site activities involve coordination with construction teams and vendors, monitoring installation progress, verifying workmanship quality, and documenting execution status to support commissioning readiness and project tracking.

Professional Impact

This ongoing LNG EPC project strengthens my expertise in site engineering workflows, vendor coordination, documentation control, and industrial safety practices within complex energy infrastructure development.
